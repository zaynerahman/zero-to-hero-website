How to choose between NEBOSH and IOSH

Choose NEBOSH if...

You want a stronger career-focused qualification with broader recognition in dedicated health and safety roles.

Choose IOSH if...

You are a manager or supervisor who wants practical workplace safety knowledge without taking a larger qualification route first.

Best for career progression

NEBOSH is generally the stronger option for learners aiming for specialist health and safety responsibilities or long-term progression.

Best for practical management use

IOSH Managing Safely is often the better fit for people who need useful safety knowledge as part of wider management responsibilities.
