How to choose the right project management certification

Choose PRINCE2 if...

You want a recognised methodology-focused certification that is valued in structured project environments and formal delivery roles.

Choose a broader certificate if...

You are newer to project work and want job-ready planning, communication, scheduling, and stakeholder management skills first.

Best for formal project frameworks

PRINCE2 is ideal for learners who want a process-driven approach to managing projects with clear roles, stages, and controls.

Best for beginners and career changers

Broader project management certificates may be the better first step if you want practical skills before moving into specialist certification pathways.
