Which belt level should you choose?

Yellow Belt

Best for beginners who want a basic introduction to Lean Six Sigma principles, terminology, and process improvement thinking.

Green Belt

Best for professionals who want to lead smaller improvement projects, use DMAIC methods, and build stronger analytical problem-solving skills.

Black Belt

Best for experienced professionals who want deeper expertise in leading complex improvement initiatives and driving wider business change.

Expert / Multi-level Program

Best for learners who want a broader long-term route covering multiple belt levels and a more comprehensive operational excellence pathway.

Best starting points

Best for beginners

Start with Yellow Belt if you are completely new to process improvement and want a lighter introduction before moving into project-led certification.

Best for career progression

Green Belt is often the strongest first serious certification for professionals who want recognised process improvement skills and practical business relevance.

Best for operations and quality roles

Lean Six Sigma certifications are especially useful for roles in operations, manufacturing, project delivery, quality management, and continuous improvement.

Best for leadership and transformation roles

Black Belt or broader expert pathways are better suited to professionals who want to lead larger improvement programs and influence organisational performance.
