How to choose the right first aid course

Choose First Aid at Work if...

You need fuller workplace coverage, more practical training time, and a qualification suited to designated first aid responsibilities.

Choose Emergency First Aid at Work if...

You need a shorter course covering essential emergency response skills for lower-risk environments or basic workplace compliance.

Best for designated first aiders

The full First Aid at Work qualification is generally the stronger option for workplaces that need a more comprehensive first aid capability.

Best for smaller teams

Emergency First Aid at Work is often the better fit for smaller workplaces or roles needing a lighter but still recognised first aid route.
